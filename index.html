    How to play

    Tap the loaf

    Every tap bakes loaves. Tap fast to fill the combo meter and multiply each tap up to x3.

    Hold for a bonus

    Once the combo meter is 40% full, press and hold the loaf to auto-bake at triple value, then get a burst bonus when you let go.

    Catch the specials

    Tap golden dough for a frenzy or lucky batch. Tap a flying doughnut to triple everything for 15 seconds.

    Grow your bakery

    Spend loaves on upgrades that bake for you, then feed the starter to restart with a permanent boost.

    What’s inside

    Five skins

    Unlock a croissant, pretzel, donut and golden loaf as your total grows.

    14 trophies

    Each one adds 1% to everything you bake.

    Sourdough prestige

    Restart for starters that boost production by 10% each.

    Saves itself

    Your progress stays in your browser, and the bakery keeps earning while you’re away.
